## 🧭 The 5 Literary Exploration Quests

We encourage you to experiment with these 5 quests in an active novel, novella, or short story project:

### Quest 1: Fostering Your First World
1. Launch the app and click **"Create New World"**.
2. Give your project a title (e.g., *The Altair Chronicles*).
3. Choose a strong master passphrase and observe the local entropy meter ("Vogon Bureaucracy Grade Security").
4. **Critical Step:** Copy and securely store your **Cosmic Recovery Key** (a 44-character key generated once to recover access if you ever lose your passphrase).
5. Finish creation and enter your newly formed world.

### Quest 2: Distraction-Free Flow & Tactile Experience
1. In the sidebar, create your first **Part**, **Chapter**, and **Scene**.
2. Begin drafting using classic literary typography (*EB Garamond*) constrained to a **physical book page ratio**.
3. Open **Preferences (`Cmd+,`)** to explore immersive focus and sensory features:
   - **Typewriter Audio:** Real acoustic typewriter audio recorded per key (including distinct spacebar, backspace, and carriage return sounds).
   - **Dynamic Focus Mode (`Cmd+Alt+F`):** Gently fades surrounding paragraphs, highlighting only the sentence you are actively typing.
   - **Zen Mode (`Cmd+Shift+F` or `F11`):** Eliminates every chrome element, window frame, and status bar, leaving only you and your words on digital paper.
   - **Smart Daily Word Goals & Pomodoro:** Strict word count computation (no inflation on copy/pasting) and timer to maintain momentum.
   - **Stylometric Statistics:** Click the bottom word counter to inspect verb density, *-ly* adverb usage, adjectives, and pacing.

### Quest 3: The Second Brain & World Dossiers
1. While writing, mention a character or place using double brackets: `[[Captain Valerius]]` or `[[Floating Citadel]]`.
2. The editor automatically transforms the token into a **bronze literary chip**.
3. Click the chip to open the entity dossier. Detail character motivations, traits, and the **dramatic arc** (Origin, Inciting Incident, Climax, and Transformation).
4. All occurrences across your manuscript are indexed and tracked in memory.

### Quest 4: The Tapestry of Time and Visual Canvas
1. Press `Cmd+Shift+T` to open the **Literary Timeline**:
   - Reorder events and compare the **Book Narrative Order** against the **Real Chronological Order**.
   - Notice automatic identification of *Flashbacks* (analepsis) and *Flashforwards* (prolepsis) integrated seamlessly into the parchment theme.
2. Press `Cmd+Shift+G` to open the **Graph & Canvas**:
   - In the **Graph**, visualize relationships between entities and spot orphan threads in your plot.
   - In the **Canvas**, create infinite conceptual boards with sticky notes and directed arrows (fully compliant with the open *JSON Canvas* standard).

### Quest 5: The Editorial Harvest (Book Parts & Direct Export)
1. Click **"Book Parts"** at the bottom of the sidebar or open the Compiler:
   - Fill in spacious editorial blocks for **Dedication**, **Epigraph**, **Foreword**, **Afterword**, **Acknowledgments**, **References & Inspirations**, and **About the Author**.
2. Select your typographic preset:
   - **Classic Novel & Narrative Fiction:** Classical first-line indent, em-dash dialogue, and ornamental scene separators.
   - **Poetry & Free Verse:** Strict line-break preservation and airy stanza pacing.
3. Export your finished work with a single click:
   - **Direct PDF Book (.pdf):** Native pure PDF formatted in A5 with running headers, paginated footers, and full international character support.
   - **Microsoft Word (.docx):** Standard submission-ready manuscript with clean pagination for publishers and agents.
   - **Digital Book (.epub):** E-book complete with an interactive table of contents and metadata, ready for Apple Books, Kobo, or Kindle.
   - **Obsidian Vault (.md):** Structured open Markdown notes with preserved wikilinks.
   - **Clean Text (.txt) + World Dossier:** Continuous plain text accompanied by a complete worldbuilding file.
